---
title: "List webinar registrations"
sidebarTitle: "List registrations"
description: "Returns who registered for a webinar, who attended and who gave feedback."
api: "GET /v1/webinars/{webinarId}/registrations"
tag: "Platinum"
---

Returns `404` if the webinar does not belong to your company.

<ParamField path="webinarId" type="string" required>
  Webinar UUID from [List webinars](/api-reference/webinars/list).
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object[]" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">Registration UUID.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="supplierId" type="string | null">The matching supplier in your supplier list, or `null` if the registrant is not one of your suppliers.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="businessName" type="string | null">Registrant's business name.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="contactEmail" type="string | null">Registrant's email.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sessionAt" type="string | null">Start of the session they booked.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="durationMinutes" type="integer | null">Session length in minutes.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="registeredAt" type="string">When they registered.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="attended" type="boolean">`true` if they attended.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="feedbackSubmitted" type="boolean">`true` once they submit the feedback form from their feedback email link.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="feedbackSubmittedAt" type="string | null">When they submitted feedback.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://api.nczgroup.com/v1/webinars/7c2e9a14-5d3b-4f8e-a1c6-2b9d4e7f0a58/registrations \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
