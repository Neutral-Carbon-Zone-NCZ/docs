---
title: "Get webinar"
description: "Returns one of your webinars."
api: "GET /v1/webinars/{webinarId}"
tag: "Platinum"
---

Returns `404` if the webinar does not belong to your company.

<ParamField path="webinarId" type="string" required>
  Webinar UUID from [List webinars](/api-reference/webinars/list).
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="id" type="string" required>Webinar UUID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="title" type="string" required>Webinar title.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="description" type="string | null">Webinar description.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="timezone" type="string | null">IANA timezone the sessions are scheduled in.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="active" type="boolean" required>`true` if the webinar is open for registration.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="firstSessionAt" type="string | null">Start of the earliest session.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="lastSessionAt" type="string | null">Start of the latest session.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="sessionCount" type="integer" required>Number of sessions.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="registeredCount" type="integer" required>Number of registrations.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="attendedCount" type="integer" required>Number of registrants who attended.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="feedbackSubmittedCount" type="integer" required>Number of registrants who submitted the feedback form.</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://api.nczgroup.com/v1/webinars/7c2e9a14-5d3b-4f8e-a1c6-2b9d4e7f0a58 \
  -H "x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
